AFAQ SYRIA PORTFOLIO GALLERY
============================
The eight portfolio cards now open dedicated galleries with ten illustrative
images each. Tapping a thumbnail opens a full-screen image viewer. The back
button returns to the portfolio index.

Images currently load from Unsplash over HTTPS and therefore require internet
access. For permanent, reliable hosting, download approved image assets and
commit them into a repository folder such as assets/portfolio/ then change the
image URL builder in index.html to local relative paths.

These images are illustrative references, not evidence of completed or owned
projects.
